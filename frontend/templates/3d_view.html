RoadGuard AI 3D Visualization

This view provides an interactive Three.js representation of the road inspection scene. Pothole markers are loaded from the selected RoadGuard AI report and displayed on the road surface.

1. Road Imagery

Road images or videos are supplied to the RoadGuard AI detection pipeline.

2. AI Detection

YOLO-based detection identifies potholes and stores their report metadata.

3. 3D Visualization

Three.js presents the road inspection scene and places the report's detected potholes as interactive markers.

4. Reconstruction

When available, the actual COLMAP reconstruction can be opened separately using the 3D Reconstruction viewer.

3D Road Monitoring

RoadGuard AI combines AI-based pothole detection with an interactive spatial visualization. Each selected report can be inspected independently, and detected potholes can be selected to view their severity, confidence and available location information.

The separate reconstruction viewer is used when a real photogrammetric reconstruction generated by the backend is available.
